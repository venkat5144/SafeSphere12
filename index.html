Emergency Details

Action-focused information

🐍

Snake Bite

Keep the person calm, reduce movement, remove tight objects, and seek professional medical assistance immediately.

⚠ Do not cut, suck, massage, or apply ice to the bite.

✓ What to do

  • Keep the person still.
  • Immobilize the limb.
  • Remove rings and tight clothing.
  • Call 112 and go to hospital.

✕ What not to do

  • Do not cut the wound.
  • Do not suck the venom.
  • Do not use a tourniquet.
  • Do not delay medical help.

👁 Symptoms

  • Pain or swelling
  • Nausea or dizziness
  • Weakness
  • Breathing difficulty

🛡 Prevention

  • Wear covered footwear.
  • Use a torch at night.
  • Clear bushes near homes.
  • Avoid handling snakes.
